## Filming notes (all three)

- Say the brand and the price out loud — many viewers listen without watching.
- Put skabbadeals.com as the first line of the video description plus the exact product link, so organic viewers can buy too.
- Keep background music low; the demo sounds (press, sizzle) sell the product.
- Film vertical-safe: keep the product in the center square so the same cut works as a Short later.
