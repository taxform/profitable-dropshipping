Privacy policy

Last updated October 30, 2026.

What we collect

When you order, we collect your email, name, shipping address and phone number to deliver your order and send updates. If you join our mailing list, we store your email address. Your cart is saved in your browser's local storage.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card details. See Stripe's privacy policy.

Who we share it with

We share your name, address and phone number only with the fulfilment partner and carrier that delivers your order. We don't sell your data.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from emails at any time, or ask us to access or delete your data by emailing hello@skabbadeals.com.
