Privacy policy

Last updated October 30, 2026.

What we collect

When you order, we collect your email, name, shipping address and phone number to deliver your order and send updates. If you join our mailing list, we store your email address. Your cart is saved in your browser's local storage.

Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card details. See Stripe's privacy policy.

Who we share it with

We share your name, address and phone number only with the fulfilment partner and carrier that delivers your order. We don't sell your data.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the shop, and Google advertising products (Google Ads and AdSense) to measure campaigns and, on our guides and homepage, to show ads. Google may use cookies to personalise ads and to measure their performance. You can learn how Google uses data from our site at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, opt out of personalised advertising at adssettings.google.com, and block cookies in your browser settings (parts of the shop, such as the cart, need local storage to work).

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from emails at any time, or ask us to access or delete your data by emailing hello@skabbadeals.com.
