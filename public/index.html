Questions

Good to know before you order

How long does delivery take?

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days and arrive in about 6–12 business days with tracking. You'll get the tracking number by email as soon as your parcel ships.

How does the bundle discount work?

Add any 2 items to your cart and every item is 10% off. Add 3 or more and it's 20% off. It applies automatically. If you also have a code, you get whichever discount is bigger.

What if it arrives damaged or I don't like it?

Tell us within 30 days of delivery. Damaged or faulty items are replaced or refunded at no cost, with no need to send them back. For change-of-mind returns we refund the item price once it's returned unused.

Do items ship together?

Gadgets ship from our fulfilment partners' warehouses, so a multi-item order can arrive in more than one parcel. Each parcel is tracked.

Which payment methods do you take?

All major cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay through Stripe's secure checkout. We never see or store your card details.
