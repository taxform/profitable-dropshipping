Timing

We add the shipping charge in the basket, and show it again at checkout. Delivery to the United States and Canada usually takes 6–15 business days after dispatch, depending on the product. Each product page shows its own estimate.

Delays and lost parcels

If your parcel hasn't arrived within 15 business days of dispatch, or if tracking shows it as delivered but you can't find it, email us with your order number. We'll check with the carrier and resend or refund as appropriate.

Business delivery

If your delivery address is a business or pickup point, tracking may show "Delivered" when the parcel reaches the front desk or concierge. If you have access problems, please check with them first and email us if it hasn't appeared within a day. Sometimes the carrier reports a delivery as "Attempted" when access was refused, even though the parcel may have been left with a building manager. This is more common with business or shared addresses but can delay things by a few business days.

Most orders arrive on the earlier side of the estimate. If yours is still on its way, start by checking the tracking link in its shipping email.
